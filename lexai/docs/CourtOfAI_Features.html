AI Legal Advisor

POST /api/advisor

Input Fields

  • situationType — 12 categories (required)
  • description — What happened (required)
  • facts — Key evidence (optional)
  • desiredOutcome — Goal (optional)
  • language — en or ur

12 Situation Types

  • Unpaid Invoice / Non-Payment
  • Contract Breach / Violation
  • Employment Dispute
  • Property / Rent Dispute
  • Business Partner Dispute
  • IP Theft / Copyright Violation
  • Consumer Complaint
  • Cyber Crime / Online Fraud
  • Family / Inheritance Matter
  • Tax / FBR Issue
  • Startup / Company Registration
  • Other Legal Matter

Output — 7-Section Legal Brief

1. Situation Summary
2–3 sentence overview
2. Legal Analysis
Applicable Pakistani law with section citations
3. Your Legal Rights
Rights and legal position
4. Recommended Actions
Numbered steps, most urgent first
5. What to Document
Evidence collection checklist
6. Draft Communication
Letter or notice template
7. When to See a Lawyer
Clear guidance on when professional representation is essential
