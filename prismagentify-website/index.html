FAQ

Questions Answered

How long does it take?
Typically 2–4 weeks from audit to go-live, depending on complexity. Simple single-agent builds can be faster; full multi-agent sales stacks with voice AI and LinkedIn integration take 4 weeks.
Do I need technical knowledge?
No. We handle everything end-to-end — scoping, building, testing, integrating, and deploying. All you need to do is show us how your team works. We'll handle the rest.
What tools do you integrate with?
HubSpot, Salesforce, Close, Pipedrive, Attio, Slack, Gmail, Apollo, Clay, and 400+ more via n8n. If you use it for sales or revenue ops, we can almost certainly integrate with it.
What if it doesn't work for us?
We start with a free audit — we only recommend a build if we can prove there's clear ROI for your specific workflow. If we can't show you the value upfront, we'll tell you honestly.
Will this break our existing workflows?
No. We build alongside your existing stack — no rip-and-replace. Your team keeps working the way they work. We layer AI automation on top, so the transition is seamless.
How is this different from just using Zapier?
Zapier connects tools. We build intelligent AI agents that reason, make decisions, and act — not just pass data between apps. Our agents understand context, personalise outreach, and take the right action based on what's actually happening in your pipeline.
